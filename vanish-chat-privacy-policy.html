Privacy Policy

Vanish Chat

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vanish Chat (Vanish Class Network). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our mobile application.

Vanish Chat is an educational communication application that allows students to exchange messages in a shared class chatroom. Messages are designed to disappear automatically 30 seconds after delivery.

2. Information We Collect

Vanish Chat may process the following information to provide its messaging functionality:

  • Messages: Text messages sent by users in the shared class chatroom.
  • Class Code: A class access code used to connect users to the appropriate chatroom.
  • Connection Information: Technical information required to establish and maintain a connection with our messaging server.
  • Device Information: Basic technical information that may be processed by the application or hosting infrastructure to maintain service functionality.

We do not ask users to provide sensitive personal information to use the application's basic messaging functionality.

3. How Messages Work

Vanish Chat is designed around temporary messaging:

  • Messages are transmitted through a shared messaging server.
  • Messages are delivered to connected participants in the class chatroom.
  • Each message is designed to disappear automatically 30 seconds after delivery.
  • The application manages message expiration on participating devices.
Important: The disappearing-message feature is not a guarantee that all copies of a message are permanently erased everywhere. Recipients may capture messages through screenshots, screen recordings, notifications, or other means. Server logs, backups, or other technical records may also retain information depending on the hosting infrastructure.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application is used to:

  • Deliver messages between class participants.
  • Connect users to the shared class chatroom.
  • Maintain communication with the messaging server.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve application reliability.
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the service.

5. Data Sharing

Messages are transmitted through our backend infrastructure to enable communication between participants.

We use third-party hosting infrastructure, including Render, to operate the messaging backend.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the application or where required by applicable law.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application.

Messages are designed to expire 30 seconds after delivery. However, we cannot guarantee that every copy, server record, backup, screenshot, or notification is immediately or permanently deleted.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information through the application.

7. Children's Privacy

Vanish Chat is designed for educational class communication. It is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party infrastructure to support its functionality.

Our backend hosting provider: Render.

Third-party providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with a revised effective date.
